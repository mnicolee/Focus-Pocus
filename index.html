Settings

Where your data is kept

Everything stays offline on your own machine. Pick the folder where Focus Pocus saves your session logs and settings.

Alert sound

If your computer is muted or very quiet when an alert goes off, Focus Pocus unmutes it and turns it up to 60% until you press "Got it", then puts it back the way it was.

Breaks

Off by default, because breaks are up to you. When it's on, pressing PAUSE asks whether you'd like a nudge to get back to work after a number of minutes you choose.

Distraction blocklist

Add a site or app by the name shown in its browser tab or title bar, like youtube, tiktok, or netflix. Focus Pocus nudges you when that word shows up in the window you're using. You can paste a link too and we'll keep just the name.
